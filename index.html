1Your location

Used to fetch the outdoor temperature where you actually are, and to work out where the sun is in your sky. Nothing is stored anywhere but this browser.

Chrome may block the request without asking. If nothing happens, click the icon at the left of the address bar and set Location to Allow, then press the button again.

2Your indoor sensor

Works with the Govee H5075 and other Govee sensors in that family. Place it in the room \u2014 it broadcasts continuously and this page only listens.

Turn Bluetooth on, then use the button below — don't add the sensor in your computer's Bluetooth settings the way you would a speaker. It isn't that kind of device, and pairing it will only drop it again.

3When sun reaches the window

No forecast knows about the building opposite you. Press the buttons the moment direct sun touches the glass and the moment it leaves, and the app learns your real skyline. Repeat every few weeks to sharpen it.
